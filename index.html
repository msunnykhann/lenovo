01. Introduction: The Lenovo Story

From Legend to Global Leader

In 1984, 11 visionary engineers led by Liu Chuanzhi founded the company in a tiny Beijing guardhouse with just $25,000, naming it "Legend" to bring digital computing power to households.

Name Origin (2003): To expand globally, they rebranded as Lenovo. "Le" honors their heritage (Legend), while "novo" is Latin for new (innovation) — symbolizing a "New Legend" combining heritage with future vision.

Logo Meaning: The iconic slanted rectangular logo symbol represents dynamic speed, agility, and forward momentum. In 2005, Lenovo acquired IBM's PC unit, establishing global presence.

Dual Headquarters Model

Operating as a global anchor with decentralized headquarters in Beijing, China (operational engine) and Morrisville, USA (corporate sales & leadership).

02. The Lenovo Odyssey: From Guardhouse to Giant

1984

Chapter 1: Out of the Guardhouse

Eleven visionary Chinese scientists, led by Liu Chuanzhi, gather inside a drafty, tiny security guardhouse in Beijing with just 200,000 yuan. They name their dream "Legend". Although their early attempts to distribute digital watches fail, they adapt, engineering a character-board that translates English OS into Chinese. The seed of a global titan is sown.

1992

Chapter 2: The Bento Box Innovation

Across the ocean in Yamato, Japan, IBM engineers design a mobile computer inspired by the simple elegance of a traditional Japanese Shokado bento box. Featuring a matte black shell and a glowing red TrackPoint button at its heart, the ThinkPad 700C is born. It goes on to redefine business computing and even journeys into outer space on NASA missions.

2005

Chapter 3: The Snake Swallows the Elephant

Legend (renamed Lenovo) acquired IBM's PC division. Critical pundits predicted failure; instead, it instantly forged the world's 3rd largest PC maker.

2013

Chapter 4: Standing at the Summit

Skeptics are silenced. Through relentless supply chain optimization and expanding its catalog with pioneering models like the convertible Yoga laptops alongside the enterprise-standard ThinkPads, Lenovo officially claims the crown: becoming the #1 PC manufacturer on Earth [Gartner & IDC, Q2 2013].

2014

Chapter 5: Building a Diversified Giant

Recognizing that the desktop computer is only one link in a larger chain, Lenovo makes two massive acquisitions in a single year: purchasing Motorola Mobility from Google to capture the mobile industry, and buying IBM's high-margin x86 server hardware business to anchor itself in corporate data centers.

2020

Chapter 6: Untethering the Laptop

Anticipating a world that works from anywhere, Lenovo introduces the Yoga 5G, the world's very first PC designed with built-in cellular 5G network speeds. Lenovo engineers successfully combine the computing power of a premium 2-in-1 notebook with the constant, high-speed connection of a mobile phone.

2026

Chapter 7: The Era of Hybrid AI

Lenovo stands at the vanguard of the next digital frontier: Hybrid AI. Powering everything from edge compute nodes and AI-equipped personal devices to massive liquid-cooled supercomputer clusters, the company records a historic $83.1 Billion in revenue. The journey from the guardhouse to the peak is complete.

2028+

Chapter 8: Future Vision & Purpose

Lenovo's roadmap is driven by Intelligent Transformation—transitioning into a service-led provider of smart solutions and carbon-neutral hybrid AI platforms.

05. ThinkVision: 20 Years of Visual Engineering

Launched in 2003, Lenovo's ThinkVision line set the industry standard for professional desktop displays. Designed to match the reliability of ThinkPad, it represents two decades of ergonomic design, pro-grade color accuracy, and zero-compromise environmental sustainability.

■ Visual Precision

Pro Color Standards: Factory-calibrated Delta E < 2 accuracy with 99% DCI-P3 wide color gamuts for graphic design and media creation.

Mini-LED Technology: High-contrast screens utilizing up to 1,152 local dimming zones for deep blacks and HDR performance.

■ User Wellness & VoIP

Ergonomic Stand: Signature red-clip stand with height, tilt, swivel, and pivot adjustments for comfort.

TUV Eyesafe: Natural low blue-light hardware filters protect eyes from fatigue without yellowing the screen display colors.

Smart VoIP: Integrated webcams with privacy shutters and single-cable 100W USB-C docking power delivery.

■ Green Engineering

Built with up to 85% Post-Consumer Recycled (PCC) plastics. Packaged in 100% plastic-free, bio-degradable FSC paper buffers with Carbon Offset services.
